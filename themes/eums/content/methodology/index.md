    content_library = 'We built our library of curated references in a sequential, controlled snow-balling method. We first did an extensive search via google scholar. We organized these references in a public Zotero library by manually assigning tags based on the content of the article, chapter or book. Multiple tags can be assigned to a single publication. These tags correspond to the categories that we represent on the website. The Zotero libraries are directly fed into the website.<br/><br/>On a yearly basis, we use an AI tool to identify any additional publications from the previous year that builds upon the sources already collected. These are reviewed and then added to the library and tagged accordingly. Beyond these central updates, we actively solicit suggestions from readers to further update our Zotero library with grey literature or publications in the member state’s native languages. If you identified a source that is missing, do not hesitate to notify us.<br/><br/>Annual updates of the website take place over the summer break.'

    content_media = 'We selected a series of (online) media sources from which to draw our news articles. These were identified with the aim of offering a representative sample. For EU news, specialized online sources are rarely embedded within a specific member state. For each of these sources, we explored the available RSS feeds from which we could draw in articles. Unless there was an explicit EU news feed, we casted the web wide. EU news often falls in the cracks between international affairs, politics, and economics. In those cases, we preferred filtering for news based on a few search terms. The full list of media channels we selected, the RSS feeds selected, as well as the search terms we use to filter any news can be found here.<br/><br/>The news is only updated every other day and is not “live”. We added a language translating tool to English.'

    content_key_actors = 'For the <i>official institutions</i>, the relevant key-actors are pre-determined. They are the official institutions through which the member states can upload their national preferences. These are the relevant governments (or coordinative ministries therein), the parliamentary committees and the permanent representation.<br/><br/>For the <i>public academics</i>, we relied on AI -assisted inquiry (Claude) to build a first overview of academics that have engaged in public debates on the EU, either in terms of analysing or commenting on recent European affairs. We particularly looked for a balanced representation (in gender and seniority). The overview of current recipients of Jean Monnet Funding was used as a starting base.<br/><br/>Identifying the <i>educational organisations</i> started from the Europe direct offices of the European Union which were complemented by a manual search for academic programmes and organisations supporting civics education the respective member state.<br/><br/>For identifying the <i>think tanks</i> we started from an inductive mapping and asked Claude to expand this list by inquiring the EU’s transparency register and by looking at political foundations.'
